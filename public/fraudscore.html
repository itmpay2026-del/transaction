How Fraud Scoring Works

  • Reason Analysis: Keywords in dispute reason affect score
  • Evidence: Lack of evidence increases risk
  • Account Age: New accounts have higher risk
  • Transaction Amount: High-value transactions are scrutinized
  • Reputation: Seller and buyer ratings influence score
  • History: Previous disputes increase risk

Risk Levels: Low (0-2), Medium (3-5), High (6+)
